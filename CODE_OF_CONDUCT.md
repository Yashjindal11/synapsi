# Code of Conduct

SynapSI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and keep disagreements focused on evidence — the same standard the framework
applies to its agents.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, and publishing others' private information.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Yashjindal11). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict
participation for violations.
