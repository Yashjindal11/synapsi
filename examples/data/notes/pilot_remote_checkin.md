# Pilot: remote check-in at two hubs (synthetic note)

This is a fictional internal note written for SynapSI examples.

During a six-week pilot at Hub North, passengers who completed remote check-in
missed 1.8% of tight connections (under 50 minutes), compared with 2.4% for
passengers checking in at the airport. The pilot group skewed towards frequent
flyers, who already miss fewer connections.

Hub South ran the same pilot and found no measurable difference (2.1% vs 2.2%),
with wide confidence intervals because only 900 pilot passengers had tight
connections.
